Free placement

You draw the path

Place your towers anywhere and they become walls. Robots always take the shortest route, so stretch it, force detours and trap them in your maze.

Evolution trees

5 towers, 5 roles

Turret, Slower, Sniper, Missile Launcher and Crusher. Each tower has two branches and seven versions to unlock.

Waves and bosses

A boss waits at the end

Fast Scouts, slow Tanks and giant bosses at the end of every chapter. Each wave is stronger than the last, from the neon factory to the heart of the machine.

Bonuses and traps

Tools to rescue a losing game

Orbital strike, EMP pulse, overload, repair and backup drone. Add mines, spikes, oil slicks, magnets and barricades to divert the robots.
